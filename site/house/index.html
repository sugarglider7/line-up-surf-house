First light · The house

Breakfast to sunset, at our house.

Line Up is our family’s house: breakfast from our kitchen every morning, two brothers between the shop, the house and the water, and a roof over the beach for the end of the day.

First light

Breakfast, made at home.

Breakfast comes with every room. Our family makes it every morning, it’s vegetarian and halal, and it changes from one day to the next. These days it’s served upstairs, with the sea in view.

Today, maybe

  • fresh orange juice
  • an omelette
  • Moroccan pancakes
  • bread
  • pastries
  • cheese
  • olives
  • honey
  • Moroccan tea or coffee

Gluten-free, or another diet? Leaving before sunrise for a flight? Tell us when you ask for dates, and we’ll do what we can.

All day

Saïd, Zohair and the family.

Line Up is run by our family: two brothers, Saïd and Zohair, between the shop, the house and the water, and the whole family behind breakfast. Someone from the family is usually close by.

Ask us about

  • A taxi into Agadir
  • A taxi to the airport
  • Day trips — Paradise Valley and more
  • Renting a car
  • A local SIM card
  • Where to eat fried fish

We speak Arabic, French and English.

Evening

Tajine, if you ask ahead.

There’s no restaurant here and no menu. But ask a day ahead and the family can usually cook a Moroccan meal at home — a chicken or kefta tajine, maybe lubya.

It’s extra; ask for today’s price. Some evenings everyone ends up at the same table.

Ask about a home-cooked meal →

Sunset

The roof, at the end of the day.

Climb past the rooms to the roof terrace. From up there you look out over the beach and the surf, and in the evening the sun goes down over the Atlantic.
