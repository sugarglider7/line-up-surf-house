01 First light

Check the water before breakfast.

Four of our eight rooms look straight over the beach to the surf. Open the window, see what the sea is doing, then head upstairs to eat.

Breakfast, made at home

Breakfast comes with every room, and our family makes it every morning. It’s vegetarian and halal, and it changes from day to day.

Gluten-free? Leaving for an early flight? Tell us, and we’ll do what we can.

Lunch at home, if you ask

Ask a day ahead and the family can usually cook a Moroccan meal for lunch or dinner — a chicken or kefta tajine, maybe lubya. It’s home cooking, not a restaurant, and it’s extra.

Anza, around the house

Anza is a quiet, local surf town on the edge of Agadir, less polished than Taghazout. There are dinosaur footprints in the rocks along the shore nearby and cafés for fried fish. Saïd is usually the one sorting out taxis, trips and tips.

04 Second session

Back out. Then back to the family.

Late in the day the boards go out again. Some evenings one of the brothers paddles out too — not a lesson, just a surf.

A family house

Line Up is our family’s house. Saïd and Zohair are brothers, the family makes breakfast, and between the shop, the house and the water someone from the family is usually close by. Ask any of us anything.

05 Sunset

Sunset from the roof.

End the day the way it started: looking at the water. From the roof terrace you look out over the beach and the surf, and the sun goes down over the Atlantic, out past the line-up. Some evenings everyone ends up at the same table.
