Afternoon · Anza

Anza, out the front door.

A quiet, local surf town on the edge of Agadir — less polished than Taghazout, and we like it that way.

Out the front door

House. Car park. Sand. Surf.

  1. Line UpThe house, the shop downstairs
  2. The car parkFree public parking
  3. Anza beachThe sand
  4. White waterWhere the waves break
  5. The line-upWhere surfers wait for the next set

There’s nothing between us and the beach but the open car park. Carry your board across and you’re on the sand. If you come by car, the public car park beside the house is free.

Afternoon

A local surf town.

Anza isn’t a resort strip. It’s a small town by the sea, with simple cafés and fried fish, and a long beach in front.

Real, not polished. Ask us where to eat.

Dinosaur footprints

Walk along the rocky shore nearby and you’ll find dinosaur footprints pressed into the rock — the oldest visitors to Anza. Ask us for the way.
